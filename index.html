About J.S. ASSOCIATES

Law for the way business works today

At J.S. Associates, we see legal practice as more than addressing a legal question. It is about understanding the circumstances behind it, recognizing what is at stake and identifying the legal course that best responds to the situation. Our work spans civil and criminal matters, family and matrimonial disputes, mediation, commercial matters and dispute resolution, with Cyber Law and Financial Law forming important areas of our practice. We particularly engage with matters where technology, finance and evolving forms of risk create legal questions that require a considered approach. By combining legal understanding with careful analysis and practical insight, we aim to provide representation that is tailored to the matter rather than driven by a standard formula. Every case has its own context, and our approach begins there.

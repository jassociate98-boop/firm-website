About our firm

J.S. Associates is a legal practice focused on helping individuals, businesses and organisations navigate legal issues arising from technology, financial activity, commercial relationships and disputes. Our approach is built around understanding the facts first, identifying the legal and practical risks, and then developing a clear course of action. Our principal areas of focus include cyber law and financial law, supported by work in commercial matters, contracts, dispute resolution and related advisory services. We aim to make complex legal issues easier to understand without losing sight of the detail that matters.
